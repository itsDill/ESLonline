20 Questions game FAQ

Quick answers before you start your animal guessing game.

How do you play 20 Questions?

Ask a yes-or-no question, use the answer as a clue, then guess the mystery animal. You can ask up to 20 questions in a round.

Can I practice English with this animal game?

Yes. Read or say questions aloud to practice English question forms and animal words, then use the written yes-or-no clues to narrow down your guess.

Is the 20 Questions game free?

Yes. Play online for free without creating an account or downloading an app.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. The game and instructions adapt to smaller screens, so you can play on a phone, tablet, or computer.
