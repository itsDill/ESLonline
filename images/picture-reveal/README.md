# Picture Reveal image pack

Drop one square or landscape image into this folder for each filename below. The game will use the matching image automatically; rounds still work with emoji placeholders while a picture is missing.

## Image checklist

### Animals

- [ ] `elephant.jpg` — elephant, clear full-body or head-and-trunk view
- [ ] `giraffe.jpg` — giraffe, ideally with neck and spots visible
- [ ] `penguin.jpg` — penguin on snow, rock, or water
- [ ] `turtle.jpg` — turtle with its shell clearly visible
- [ ] `butterfly.jpg` — colorful butterfly on a flower or leaf
- [ ] `dolphin.jpg` — dolphin in blue water
- [ ] `fox.jpg` — orange fox, preferably outdoors
- [ ] `owl.jpg` — owl with its face and eyes visible

### Food

- [ ] `watermelon.jpg` — cut watermelon showing the red inside
- [ ] `pineapple.jpg` — whole pineapple with its leafy top
- [ ] `pancakes.jpg` — a stack of pancakes, ideally with fruit or syrup
- [ ] `sushi.jpg` — a small colorful selection of sushi
- [ ] `ice-cream.jpg` — ice cream cone or cup with a clear scoop
- [ ] `strawberry.jpg` — close-up of ripe strawberries

### Nature

- [ ] `rainbow.jpg` — bright rainbow over a landscape
- [ ] `volcano.jpg` — recognizable volcano, with or without a small eruption
- [ ] `waterfall.jpg` — waterfall surrounded by nature
- [ ] `mountains.jpg` — mountain range with a clear peak

### Places & travel

- [ ] `lighthouse.jpg` — lighthouse beside the sea
- [ ] `castle.jpg` — recognizable castle with towers
- [ ] `hot-air-balloon.jpg` — hot-air balloon in the sky

### Transport

- [ ] `bicycle.jpg` — bicycle in side view
- [ ] `train.jpg` — train on tracks, with engine visible
- [ ] `sailboat.jpg` — sailboat on open water with its sail raised

### Everyday objects

- [ ] `umbrella.jpg` — open, colorful umbrella
- [ ] `alarm-clock.jpg` — traditional alarm clock with its face visible
- [ ] `camera.jpg` — recognizable camera, front or three-quarter view
- [ ] `backpack.jpg` — backpack, ideally against a simple background

### Sports

- [ ] `soccer-ball.jpg` — classic soccer ball, close enough to see its panels
- [ ] `tennis.jpg` — tennis ball and racket, or a close-up of a tennis ball

## Recommended image specs

- Use JPG photos with the exact `.jpg` filenames above.
- Aim for at least 900 × 600 px, landscape (16:9 is ideal); the game crops with `object-fit: cover`.
- Keep the key subject near the center so the tile reveal works well on phones and tablets.
- Prefer bright, uncluttered images with one obvious subject and no text or watermarks.
- Use images you own, created, or have permission to publish; save a note of the source and license for each image.
- Compress photos to roughly 250–500 KB where possible. The game provides emoji placeholders for files that have not been added yet.
